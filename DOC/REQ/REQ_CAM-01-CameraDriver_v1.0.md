# CAM-01 - Camera Driver
## Overview
The camera must be able to follow the player and dynamically move across the level.
## Requirements
### Follow
The camera must be able to smoothly follow the player throughout the level.
### Dynamic Movement
The camera must be able to move towards where the player's crosshair is aiming.
### Shake
The camera must be able to be shake for certain actions (e.g. player shooting)
